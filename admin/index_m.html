Serial Permission Hint

If the device shows up but the adapter still logs Permission denied, the ioBroker user usually needs access to the serial device and membership in dialout.

chgrp dialout /dev/ttyACM0 chmod 660 /dev/ttyACM0 usermod -aG dialout iobroker
